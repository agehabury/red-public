## [RED's View]
The most significant development is the ongoing Phase 1 trial of emicizumab in severe VWD. Emicizumab's mechanism of action, mimicking activated Factor VIII, could offer a subcutaneous prophylactic option for VWD patients, particularly those with Type 3 VWD who often have very low or absent VWF and FVIII levels. This could potentially reduce the burden of frequent intravenous infusions associated with current VWF/FVIII replacement therapies. However, it is crucial to note this is a Phase 1 study, and clinical efficacy and safety in VWD are yet to be established. The completed study on gingival bleeding (NCT03078595) is less impactful without published results, but could provide valuable data on a common VWD symptom.
